Last updated: 19 October 2026

This page explains how this website (helloivo.pt) uses cookies and handles personal data. It's kept intentionally short and focused on what's actually collected here: this site doesn't sell products, run ads, or process payments.

What this site collects

The only data collected is through Google Analytics (GA4), a web analytics service that helps understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and for how long. Google Analytics is only activated if you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. If you click "Decline", or close the banner without choosing, no analytics cookies are set and no data is sent to Google Analytics. This site has no forms, accounts or payments. If you email or call me, I only use your details to reply.

Cookies used

When active, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (typically named _ga and _ga_*) used to distinguish visitors and sessions, which typically last up to two years. See Google's own Privacy Policy for details on how this data is processed.

Legal basis

Analytics cookies and data are processed only on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 5 of Portuguese Law 41/2004). You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it; withdrawing does not affect processing done before.

How long data is kept

Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in the Google Analytics property, which is no longer than 14 months. Cookies expire after the periods set by Google, and are removed from this site when you decline or withdraw consent.

Third parties and transfers

If you accept analytics, your data is processed by Google, which may transfer it outside the European Economic Area (including to the United States) under safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. Independently of cookies, this site also loads some images and video thumbnails from Unsplash, Pexels and YouTube (fonts are hosted on this site); those providers receive your IP address as part of delivering that content. Videos are not played on this site: they open on YouTube in a new tab.

Your choices and rights

You can change your cookie choice at any time using the button below or the "Cookie preferences" link in the footer. You also have the right to access, rectify or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, email ivosousamartins@gmail.com.

Complaints

If you believe your data is handled unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), at www.cnpd.pt.
