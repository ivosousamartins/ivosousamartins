Hello, I'm Ivo.

I work at the intersection of brand strategy, creative production and digital communication, helping brands show up with purpose across every channel.

Over 20+ years I've worked with luxury travel brands, restaurant groups, energy companies, city councils and national political parties, to name a few, on billboards, websites, social feeds and election campaigns, including the visual identity for two of Portugal's most-followed political campaigns in 2024.

I enjoy having a helicopter view on things, then drilling down to what matters: strategic thinking and hands-on ideation and design, from first brief to final delivery.

Services

It has never been easier to produce, and never harder to be remembered. Anyone can generate a logo, a video, a landing page or a piece of content in minutes. I think I've done well at making sure all of those outputs can hold a strong point of view and build brand equity.

Strategic planning and creative strategy decide where a brand should play and what makes it worth noticing.

Brand strategy and creative direction turn that decision into a world: a tone, a look, a way of moving.

Design carries it everywhere attention lives: feeds, pages, screens, events.

Web and UX give the brand a home built to convert, not just impress.

And content, video, and presentations make your voice heard with any audience.

I've done the math and can safely say there is not one single discipline that matters most. The key is how to make all of it work together: a plan that shapes an idea, an idea that shapes a film, a film that feeds the feed, a feed that lands on a page, a page that is able to attract and convert, and a healthy brand as a result.

I've had the fortune (along with some hard work) to be at the core of some really cool ideas, campaigns, projects and organisations, in settings where I was able to connect the dots in brand building, marketing development and integrated comms.

Making things feels cheaper these days (depending on how many tokens you spend). I feel I can add the right energy, tools, craft and experience, wherever this is all headed!
